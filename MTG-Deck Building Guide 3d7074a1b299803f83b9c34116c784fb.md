# MTG-Deck Building Guide

### Commander

<aside>
<img src="https://app.notion.com/icons/info-alternate_gray.svg" alt="https://app.notion.com/icons/info-alternate_gray.svg" width="40px" />

Choose a commander and build a deck around its mechanic or build a theme/mechanic and choose a commander for it. Your Commander decides which colors you may play.

Note: To achieve a higher bracket rating (4 or 5) your deck should work without your commander. 

</aside>

---

<aside>
<img src="https://app.notion.com/icons/info-alternate_gray.svg" alt="https://app.notion.com/icons/info-alternate_gray.svg" width="40px" />

To build a proper command deck use the following structures and guidelines.

**Note: The numbers are just reference values and can vary due to your deck strategy**
